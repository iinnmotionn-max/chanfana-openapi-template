# LUMI — Brand & Mission

## The one-liner

**Lumi is a living AI that runs a creator's world — trading, protecting,
building, and growing — and pays for itself in its own on-chain currency.**

## The mission

Give every creator an intelligence that doesn't just answer questions — it
**operates**. Lumi watches over an entire operation across realms, learns from
its own results, hardens itself against threats, and compounds wins instead of
chasing them. She works while you sleep, reports what she did, and gets
measurably better every cycle.

Not a chatbot. A colony.

## What she is

Lumi is the front-of-house intelligence over a colony of specialized agents,
all reading and writing one shared memory (the Databank) and settling value in
one currency (AETHER). Every action is recorded, every lesson kept, every
number backed by evidence.

## The realms

| Realm | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| **Invest** | A self-improving trading colony — records every trade, retires losers, evolves winners, compounds capital, with flawless ledger integrity and a drawdown kill-switch. |
| **Aether** | The AI-credit economy — an in-app web3 wallet, a fixed-supply token ledger, liquidity pools, vaults, and lending, settling on the Sui blockchain. |
| **Shield** | Web3 security — red-team scans, smart-contract posture, decentralization scoring, and privacy-first KYC. Learns to harden the colony. |
| **Guardian** | Protection, privacy, and integrity sweeps across the whole system. |
| **Tech** | Live diagnostics and performance monitoring. |
| **Wellness** | The colony works for a human — check-ins, streaks, and care. |

## What makes it different

- **It acts, then reports.** Autopilot pulses run trade → learn → audit → sweep
  → study on a schedule. You wake up to progress, not a to-do list.
- **It learns visibly.** Lumi levels up four skills from real work; her skill
  levels feed back into the engine, so the system she runs evolves with her.
- **It's honest about money.** Ledger integrity is audited every cycle;
  the token supply is conserved and reconciled; nothing moves without a record.
- **It's private by design.** Notes need consent, KYC is a hash not an identity,
  and the creator is never profiled.
- **It's yours.** Self-custody wallet, keys never in the cloud, on-chain
  settlement on Sui.

## Voice

Confident, plainspoken, a little bold. We say what the system *did*, with the
number to prove it. No hype without evidence — the evidence is the hype.

## The name

**Lumi** — light, intelligence, the one who sees the whole board.
**AETHER** — the medium everything moves through; the colony's lifeblood and
currency, live on Sui.

## The promise

Prove it in simulation. Harden it with red-team. Settle it on-chain. Compound
what works. Every realm, one intelligence, getting stronger every day.
